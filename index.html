Frequently Asked Questions

Is my feedback really 100% anonymous?
Yes. When you choose "Submit Anonymously", your name, roll number, and student identifier are scrubbed before reaching the faculty dashboard. Only aggregated statistics and anonymous commentary are surfaced to professors and department heads.
Can faculty members see who gave specific low ratings?
No. Faculty members only view aggregated averages across the 5 dimensions (Teaching Methodology, Syllabus Coverage, Lab Support, Doubt Resolution, and Grading Fairness). They cannot trace individual scores back to any student.
Can I edit my feedback after submission?
Yes! You can view and update your submitted feedback from the "Feedback History" tab anytime before the semester evaluation deadline (5 August).
What happens if I miss the feedback deadline?
Submitting semester feedback is mandatory for releasing final semester grade cards and hall tickets on the university portal. Please ensure all pending courses are evaluated before the deadline.
How is student feedback used by the academic board?
The Academic Committee reviews departmental feedback biannually. Scores directly influence faculty teaching development, laboratory equipment upgrades, syllabus revisions, and teaching methodology improvements.

Guidelines for Constructive Feedback

  • Be Specific: Mention particular units, assignments, or teaching techniques that helped or hindered your learning.
  • Focus on Academics: Frame comments around course structure, lab mentorship, pacing, and clarity rather than personal traits.
  • Offer Solutions: If a lab needs more practical coding sessions, recommend adding interactive workshops or practice tasks.
